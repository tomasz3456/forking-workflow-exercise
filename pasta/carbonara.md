# Spaghetti alla Carbonara

Simple, classic Roman carbonara — eggs, cured pork, hard cheese, black pepper. No cream.

## Servings
4

## Time
Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min · Total 25 min

## Ingredients
- 400 g spaghetti (or rigatoni)
- 150 g guanciale, diced (or pancetta if unavailable)
- 3 large eggs + 1 large egg yolk
- 100 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated (or mix Pecorino + Parmesan)
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Salt (for pasta water)

## Equipment
- Large pot
- Large skillet
- Mixing bowl
- Tongs or pasta fork
- Grater

## Method
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Cook pasta until very al dente (1–2 min less than package).
2. Meanwhile, heat the skillet over medium and cook guanciale until fat renders and edges are crisp (6–8 min). Remove from heat but keep warm; leave fat in pan.
3. In a bowl, whisk eggs, yolk and grated cheese until smooth. Season generously with black pepper.
4. Reserve 1–2 cups (240–480 ml) pasta cooking water, then drain pasta.
5. Add hot pasta to the skillet with guanciale and toss to coat in fat. Let cool 30–60 seconds so eggs won’t scramble.
6. Pour egg + cheese mixture over pasta and toss vigorously. Add reserved pasta water a little at a time to emulsify into a silky sauce that coats the pasta.
7. Adjust seasoning with cheese and pepper. Serve immediately with extra Pecorino and pepper.

## Tips
- Work off direct heat when combining eggs to avoid scrambling.
- Use guanciale and Pecorino for the most authentic flavor.
- Sauce should be glossy and cling to the pasta; thin with pasta water, not oil or cream.

## Variations
- Pancetta or smoked bacon if guanciale is unavailable.
- Use whole eggs only for a lighter sauce; extra yolks make it richer.

Enjoy immediately.
